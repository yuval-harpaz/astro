JWST Color Lab

How to make a color image

  1. Find an object in the list on the left: browse by week with ◀ 7 days, or search by target, title or filter. Each object's images are sorted from long to short wavelength.
  2. Check R for the long wavelength image(s), G for the middle and B for the short ones. One or more per color; without G, green is the average of R and B, like the bot. Auto RGB next to an object name selects what the bot would choose.
  3. Press Compose. The first time takes 10–30 s per image: only the science layer is downloaded and reduced to the chosen size.
  4. Tune on the right: stretch factor, limits, log or asinh, filt05 colors, reference grid, or nudge a misaligned layer. These re-compose in about a second. Zoom with the mouse wheel or + / −, drag to pan; ▭ Limits from area (or Shift+drag) takes the limits from a selected area, e.g. a galaxy nucleus. SIMBAD labels known objects; type a filter such as NGC or G (galaxies).
  5. Save as JPG or PNG, or download a Local script that does the same at full resolution on your computer.

Everything runs in your browser (Python via Pyodide); nothing is uploaded. Progress is printed in the log at the bottom right.
