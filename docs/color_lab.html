JWST Color Lab

How to make a color image

  1. Choose JWST and / or HST at the top left, or single instruments (e.g. only MIRI). Find an object in the list: browse by week with ◀ 7 days, or search by target, title, filter or position (RA, Dec; names not in the list are looked up in SIMBAD). Once you choose the first image, the list switches to images of the same sky area from all telescopes, instruments and target names (✕ Exit returns to the previous list; r sets the radius). Each object's images are sorted from long to short wavelength. Selected (next to All) shows only the checked images.
  2. Check R for the long wavelength image(s), G for the middle and B for the short ones. One or more per color; without G, green is the average of R and B. Auto RGB next to an object name chooses for you: the reddest image as R, the bluest as B and the middle one as G, from images of the same pointing.
  3. Press Compose. The first time takes 10–30 s per image: only the science layer is downloaded and reduced to the chosen size.
  4. Tune on the right: stretch factor, limits, log or asinh, filt05 colors, reference grid, or nudge a misaligned layer or a whole color (Show R / G / B under the nudge buttons hides colors to check the alignment). These re-compose in about a second. Zoom with the mouse wheel or + / −, drag to pan; ▭ Limits from area (or Shift+drag) takes the limits from a selected area, e.g. a galaxy nucleus. SIMBAD labels known objects; type a filter such as NGC or G (galaxies).
  5. Save as JPG or PNG, or download a Local script that does the same at full resolution on your computer. Download FITS saves the checked images themselves (header and science layer, full resolution, unprocessed).

Everything runs in your browser (Python via Pyodide); nothing is uploaded. Progress is printed in the log at the bottom right.
